What we build

Four things.
Done properly.

Saunas

Interior and outdoor saunas built the Finnish way. Proper ventilation, correct vapour barriers, and panelling that stays straight for decades.

  • New builds and full renovations
  • Benches, panelling, stove installation prep
  • Outdoor sauna buildings and yard saunas

Deckings

Terraces and decks with proper foundations and framing. The part you don't see is the part that decides how long it lasts.

  • Ground-level and raised terraces
  • Railings, stairs and pergolas
  • Wood and composite surfaces

Facades

New cladding and facade renovations that protect the structure underneath. Ventilation gaps, flashings and details done right, then paint that lasts.

  • Timber cladding, new and replacement
  • Windows, trims and flashings
  • Surface treatment and painting

Roofs

Roof renewals and repairs for the Nordic climate. Snow loads, ice and driving rain are considered in every detail from underlay to ridge.

  • Full roof renewals, sheet and tile
  • Underlays, battens and ventilation
  • Gutters, snow guards and safety equipment
How we work

From first call
to handover.

01

Site visit

We come and look at the actual site: measurements, structures, access. No quoting blind from photos.

02

Fixed quote

A written quote with materials, labour and schedule spelled out. What we quote is what you pay.

03

The build

We show up when agreed, keep the site tidy, and keep you informed. Weather-critical stages are protected.

04

Handover

Walkthrough together, punch list closed, site cleaned. You get documentation of what was built and how.

Why Xpress Bygg

Small team.
Serious standards.

Everything we build lives outside, in Finnish weather, through Finnish winters. That shapes how we work: moisture management, ventilation and structural detail come first, finish comes second, and both are done properly.

We're deliberately a small team. The people who quote your project are the people who build it. You always know who is on your site and who to call.
